## Tips for First Jobs

1. **Apply to 5-10 jobs per day** when starting
2. **Write custom proposals** — never copy-paste
3. **Mention your GitHub repo** in every proposal — it's proof you can deliver
4. **Start with small gigs** to get 5-star reviews fast
5. **Deliver early** and over-deliver (add a bonus analysis or visualization)
6. **Ask for reviews** politely after delivery
